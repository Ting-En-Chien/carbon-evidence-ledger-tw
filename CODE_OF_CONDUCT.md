# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Carbon Evidence Ledger respectful,
professional, and welcoming for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- give specific, constructive feedback about work rather than people;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect privacy, intellectual property, and source attribution;
- use synthetic or properly anonymized examples;
- avoid presenting prototype output as professional compliance advice;
- help maintain an environment where beginners can ask questions safely.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- deliberate intimidation, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- posting credentials, personal information, or confidential company data;
- knowingly misrepresenting calculations, sources, regulatory status, or
  project limitations;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in Issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions,
documentation, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not include private or sensitive details in a public Issue. Use GitHub's
private content-reporting tools for conduct concerns. Potential security
vulnerabilities must be reported through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement will
consider context, impact, prior behavior, and whether corrective action was
taken.

Good-faith reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.
